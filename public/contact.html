Is the stone in the photo the one I get?

Yes. Every listing is a single, specific piece photographed as it is, unless the listing says otherwise.

How do you ship?

Every order is hand-wrapped, cushioned and insured. You'll get tracking as soon as it's on its way.

How should I care for my crystals?

Dust gently with a soft dry cloth. Keep softer or coloured stones like amethyst, rose quartz and fluorite out of long direct sunlight so they don't fade.

Can you find a specific stone for me?

Often, yes. Send us a message with what you're hoping for and we'll keep an eye out on our next sourcing trip.
