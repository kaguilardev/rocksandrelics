Where it began

A love of stones, turned into a craft.

✦

Stardust & Stoneworks started with a simple love of stones: the weight of them in your palm, the colour, the way light moves through them. What began as a personal collection grew into a small studio sharing those finds.

We work directly with trusted miners and carvers and choose every piece ourselves. No catalogues, no lucky dips. If it's on our shelves, we've held it.
